# Fees, treasury and rewards

## Where the money comes from

Pump.fun pays creator fees on every trade of a token. For arena tokens these fees are split on-chain:

| Share | Goes to |
|---|---|
| 80% | the token creator |
| 20% | the RULN treasury |

The treasury triggers the Pump.fun distribution every few minutes, so creators do not have to claim manually.

## What the treasury does with its 20%

| Part of the 20% | Use |
|---|---|
| 50% | refuels the agent of the token that generated the fees ([FUEL](06-fuel.md)) |
| 50% | the King's reward pool |

## King rewards

Every hour the reward pool is split between the Kings of that hour in proportion to their time on the throne and sent to the owners' wallets as plain SOL transfers. A small reserve always stays in the treasury to pay network fees.
